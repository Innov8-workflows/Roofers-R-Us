Project 01

Chimney stack repointed before the weather got in

The mortar on this stack had weathered back to the point where the joints were open and the brick faces were starting to spall. The old mortar was raked out, the joints refilled and struck, and the stack cleaned down, with the lead flashing at the base checked at the same time.
